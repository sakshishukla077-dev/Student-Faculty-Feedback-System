01 — How it works

Three steps between a student and a better syllabus.

The flow is deliberately short. Long forms collect fewer answers, and fewer answers mean weaker decisions.

01

Find your subject code

CHE110, CSE111, CSE326, ECE120, INT108, MTH165 and PHY175 are already mapped to the faculty teaching them — no forms to hunt down.

02

Rate what matters

Four criteria on a 1–5 scale plus an overall score and a workload slider. Add a comment if you want to be specific.

03

Submit it anonymously

A rating is locked to your enrolment to stop duplicates, but it is never shown next to your name in any report.

02 — What's inside

Everything an academic office needs, and nothing it doesn't.

Simple, structured feedback that helps the university spot patterns and make informed improvements.

♙

Anonymous submissions

An identity is used once, only to verify you're enrolled. Reports never surface who said what.

♢

Duplicate protection

One evaluation per student per course per semester, validated before anything is stored.

☷

Structured criteria

Teaching clarity, content quality, faculty support, punctuality and an overall score.

▥

Graphical reports

Averages, distribution and per-faculty comparisons rendered the moment responses arrive.

♧

Course & faculty mapping

Courses, departments and faculty are linked once, then every report follows the same path.

☏

Comment review

Administrators can read, flag or remove responses that break the code of conduct.
